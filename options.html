Start with an offset of 0. Check the size before printing after changing rolls. D11H prints a band about 12.2 mm wide on 14–15 mm tape.

Brewfather

An API key with Read Batches permission is required.

Your access details are stored only in this Chrome extension. Requests go directly to Brewfather. Edit Batches permission is not required.

First run

  1. Save your Brewfather access details and the size of the installed label roll.
  2. Open Batches and click “Print label” on a batch to open its preview.
  3. Optionally turn on Add QR for that job, set the number of copies, click “Print”, and choose the printer in Chrome’s Bluetooth dialog.

While the tab and connection stay open, the next label prints from the preview panel. Chrome may ask you to choose the printer again after a page reload.
